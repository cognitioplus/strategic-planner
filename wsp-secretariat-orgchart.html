⚠️ The Generalist Trap & Structural Ambiguity

Current State: Only 4 plantilla positions out of 9+ roles. Non-permanent staff fill functional voids, creating categorical overlaps between admin, technical, and support.

Archetype: This reflects the Generalist Trap—agility appears valuable but prevents deep expertise and accountability.

Risk: Institutional memory loss, burnout, and fragility during leadership transitions.

Solution: Transition to calibrated structure with permanent roles in both Administrative and Technical Groups. See Calibrated Structure.
