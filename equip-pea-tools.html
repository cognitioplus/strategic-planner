The Methodology: A Four-Stage Execution Plan

EQUIP Political Economy Analysis (PEA) Diagnostic Suite

Stage 1
Mobilization (Inception & System Mapping)
Stage 2
Fieldwork (Data Collection)
Stage 3
Analysis & Drafting (Synthesizing Findings)
Stage 4
Validation (Refinement & Finalization)

Stage 1: Mobilization & System Mapping

Rapid desk review (Regime-Niche framework) and defining 'EQUIP readiness' criteria.

Regime-Niche-Landscape Matrix
Digital / Manual

Categorizes stakeholders to understand regional dynamics of change vs. stability.

Landscape (External Context) Macro-level pressures: Economic shifts, political trends, and labor market demands pressuring the system.
Regime (Status Quo) Actors: RDCs, DepEd/TESDA Regional Offices, Business Chambers.
Role: Maintain existing institutional habits.
Niche (Innovators) Actors: Outliers, localized NGO initiatives.
Role: Operating outside dominant rules; ready to champion transformation.
Rapid Desk Review Synthesis
Template

Designed to bridge the gap between national data and regional realities.

  • National E2E Trends: Summary of curriculum gaps and labor market failures.
  • Regional Contexts: Sensitive data on political settlements and governance structures.
  • Baseline Readiness: Preliminary indicators of political will in the 6 target regions.

Stage 2: Field Data Collection

Testing hypotheses on actor incentives. Focus on informal rules and local policy dynamics.

Systemic Readiness Template
Qualitative Diagnostic

Filters for "partnership potential" vs technical capacity.

Political Will: Willingness to challenge norms and pilot solutions.
Partnership Capacity: Ability to work with LGUs & Industry.
Niche Potential: Existence of pre-conditions for prototyping.
KII & FGD Guides
Semi-Structured

Decodes informal rules and implementation dynamics.

  • 1. Power Probes: Uncover elite capture and power structures.
  • 2. Incentive Alignment: Why is private sector hesitant?
  • 3. Conflict Sensitivity: Navigating regional political settlements safely.
Transcription Services
Digital Strategy

Essential for the 6-region scope. Captures the nuance of regional narratives and "informal institutional habits" often revealed through subtle storytelling, preserving them for the AII Matrix.

Stage 3: Analysis & Drafting

Synthesizing findings. Identifying Ecosystem Enablers using 'Actors-Institutions-Ideas' framework.

Actors-Institutions-Ideas (AII) Matrix
Analytical Framework
Component Analysis Focus
Actors Interests and incentives of state/non-state stakeholders.
Institutions Formal rules vs. Informal norms ("Rules of the Game").
Ideas Narratives and habits justifying the status quo.
Systemic Constraint Map
The "Iceberg" Model
Visible Symptoms (Above Water) Curriculum gaps, lack of facilities.
Hidden Lock-ins (Underwater) Informal power structures, elite capture, political settlements.

Stage 4: Validation & Finalization

Iterative Workshop to test findings, build ownership, and refine the path forward.

Stakeholder Validation Workshop
Online Platform

Iterative political engagement mechanism for regional actors.

Feedback Loops
Confirms if analysis of power structures resonates with local experience.
Feasibility Testing
Discusses if policy pathways are politically viable for implementers.
Consensus-Building Tracker
Management Tool

Refines recommendations by addressing conflicting viewpoints.

Resistance Mapping
Tracks risks & triggers for status-quo actors.
Ownership Indicator
Records consensus points to build reform ownership.
